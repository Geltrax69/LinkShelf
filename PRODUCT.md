# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
Native macOS (the skill's web/iOS/Android labels do not describe this product).

## Stack
Explicitly specified: Swift, SwiftUI, macOS 14+, SwiftData, WidgetKit, App Intents and LinkPresentation. No Electron or third-party runtime dependencies without justification.

## Users and Purpose
People organizing saved websites, documentation, videos, design references and research into visual workspaces. Save → organize → preview → search → open.

## Capabilities and Constraints
The blueprint specifies folders with custom icons, visual link previews, local search, favorites, drag/drop and configurable desktop widgets. These are planned features, not claims about the initial shell. No accounts, analytics, custom backend or cloud dependency.

## Brand Commitments
LinkShelf, native Mac conventions, SF Symbols, system typography, semantic colors, restrained motion and no fake glass or web dashboard chrome. Preserve the supplied three-area layout direction.

## Evidence on Hand
`LinkShelf_macOS_Project_Blueprint.md` supplies the product, stack, design direction and exact first-session scope. No existing implementation or brand assets were supplied.

## Accessibility
Keyboard navigation, labels for important controls, system appearance, contrast and reduced-motion support are required by the blueprint.
